# SmartLens — Scope Document

## 1. Project Summary
SmartLens is a 4-week self-managed project to design and deliver an AI-powered 
Power BI dashboard that helps SME business owners in Zimbabwe understand their 
financial health, forecast cash flow, and receive plain-English AI-generated 
insights — without needing a financial analyst.

## 2. Problem Being Solved
Most SMEs in Zimbabwe manage finances manually or with basic spreadsheets. 
They have no visibility into future cash flow, no way to spot spending anomalies 
early, and no affordable access to financial intelligence tools.

## 3. In Scope
- Simulated SME financial dataset (12 months of revenue, expenses & transactions)
- Data cleaning & preparation in Excel
- Power BI data model (relationships, DAX measures, calculated columns)
- Financial Overview page
- Cash Flow Forecast page (30/60/90-day forecast)
- Expense Breakdown page
- AI Insights page (LLM-generated summaries & anomaly flags)
- Published to Power BI Service
- Full PM artifact suite
- Portfolio write-up on Notion and GitHub

## 4. Out of Scope
- Live database or ERP connections
- Real client data
- Mobile application
- Multi-user or role-based access
- Paid tools or software

## 5. Constraints
- 4 weeks hard deadline
- Solo project — one person handles PM, data, and development
- $0 budget — all tools must be free tier
- No access to real business data

## 6. Assumptions
- Power BI Desktop (free) supports all required visualisations
- A free LLM API is sufficient for generating AI insight summaries
- Simulated data modelled on realistic SME financial patterns is credible
- All tools are accessible from Zimbabwe

## 7. Change Management
Any changes to scope follow this process:
1. Identify the proposed change
2. Assess impact on timeline and deliverables
3. Decide — accept, defer to v2, or reject
4. Document the decision in the retrospective

*Default position on new ideas during the 4-week sprint: defer to v2.*
